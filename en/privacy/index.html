3. Submissions, comments and information you provide

Online submissions store the project name, URL, recommendation and submission time for manual review and listing. Project comments store your nickname, text, any platform or experience details you provide, and submission time; approved comments are displayed publicly. Please do not include passwords, API keys, private conversations or other people's non-public information.

If you use a community account, the site also stores your username, password-verification data and login sessions. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, and cookies maintain login state. Posts and replies submitted while signed in to the community are displayed publicly upon submission.

4. Visit statistics and abuse prevention

Servers process HTTP information such as IP address, browser type, request path and time to deliver pages and downloads and protect the site. Likes, submissions and comments use salted IP hashes to help deduplicate requests and limit abuse. The production site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate visit statistics, and download statistics aggregate requests and transfer volume; these counts do not represent actual user numbers.

5. Third-party services and external links

Features such as submissions and comments use Alibaba Cloud CAPTCHA or Cloudflare Turnstile depending on the deployment; verification services may process request and device information. Project, GitHub and friend links lead to third-party sites governed by their own privacy policies. Listings and friend links provide navigation and do not imply affiliation or mutual endorsement.

6. Corrections, removal and data deletion

Authors or other rightsholders may request corrections or removal of a listing. You may also contact us to request changes to or deletion of your comments, submissions or account-related data. Please provide the relevant link and enough information to verify the request. We will review and handle it; do not post sensitive information in a public issue.
