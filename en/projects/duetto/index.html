PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Duetto is a self-deployable player for two people to listen to songs together. It allows people and AI to share the playback queue and current songs, and save the history of joint listening.

The project emphasizes that AI has actually read the playback records, rather than just simulating listening based on song titles.

  • Suitable for:hopes to turn music into a human-machine companion for long-term joint activities.
  • Platform:Self-hosted Web.
  • Usage:Deploy the player and connect the companion to its songs and history data.
  • License:MIT。
