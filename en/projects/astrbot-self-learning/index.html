PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

AstrBot Self Learning is a plug-in that allows robots to learn autonomously from long-term conversations. It analyzes speaking styles, group slang and member interactions, applying the results to more natural responses.

The plug-in also maintains social relationships and favorability, and allows the personality to gradually evolve with communication. The group chat content will enter the learning process, and group members should be informed and confirm data boundaries before deployment.

  • Suitable for:hope that AstrBot will gradually adapt to users with fixed group languages ​​and social relationships.
  • Platform:AstrBot plugin.
  • Usage:After installation, configure the learning scope, model and relationship parameters, and clarify the boundaries of group chat data usage.
