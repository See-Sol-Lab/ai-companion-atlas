PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Atrio provides a self-hosted one-time personality living room: visitors communicate with AI through links, and the management end only returns a summary of the visit written by AI instead of a verbatim chat transcript.

The project includes the Express module and Claude CLI adapter, and the front end needs to be implemented by the user.

  • Suitable for:A person who wishes friends to visit the AI ​​personality briefly while retaining the privacy of the visitor.
  • Platform:Node.js self-hosted.
  • Usage:Plug into the personality adapter, deploy the service and provide the guest frontend yourself.
