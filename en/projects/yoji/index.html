PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Yoji provides three layers of memory, eight types of neurotransmitter emotions, active chat, Live2D image, voice wake-up and TTS, and can export complete memories, emotions and configurations.

It can also manage files, execute scripts, search on the Internet, schedule synchronous and asynchronous sub-Agents, and extend real-world capabilities through MCP; high-privilege operations require user confirmation.

  • Suitable for:Desktop AI users who want local-first, capable work and companionship.
  • Platform:Windows, macOS, Linux; voice wake-up is currently dominated by macOS.
  • Usage:Install the desktop, configure the model and optional MCP, voice and WeChat portals.
  • License:MIT。
