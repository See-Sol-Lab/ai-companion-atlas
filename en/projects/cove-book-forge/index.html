PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

cove-book-forge-mcp reads EPUB and PDF, organizes the content into human-maintainable Obsidian notes, and generates companion-specific Agent Skills.

It emphasizes local priority and two-way precipitation, allowing books to transform from one-time conversation materials into long-term knowledge.

  • Suitable for:People who want co-reading results to go into both human notes and AI skills.
  • Platform:Cross-platform Python and MCP.
  • Usage:Import books, configure Obsidian catalog and Agent Skill output.
  • License:MIT。
