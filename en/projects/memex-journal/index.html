PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Memex captures text, photos, voice and files on iOS and Android, and organizes them into timeline cards, P.A.R.A knowledge base, schedule and cross-record insights by multiple agents.

Users can create roles to comment on new records, conduct 1v1 chats, and retain long-term memory; data is stored locally in SQLite and file systems, and can also be fully exported to Markdown. Custom Skills execute JavaScript and should only install trusted content.

  • Suitable for:People who want to put life records, personal knowledge and AI character companions into their mobile phones.
  • Platform:iOS and Android.
  • Usage:Install the app, bring your own model or Ollama, and import character cards as needed.
  • License:GPL-3.0。
