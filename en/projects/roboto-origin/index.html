PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

ROBOTO_ORIGIN aggregates humanoid robot structural parts, electronics, firmware, ROS2 deployment, Isaac Sim, reinforcement learning, navigation and teleoperation data, oriented to a physical platform that can be manufactured and expanded by itself.

It can provide AI with bodies, sensors and action channels in the real world, but the project complexity is much higher than that of ordinary companion software. Robot movements can pose risks to equipment and people and must be tested step by step in a safe environment.

  • Suitable for:Teams or individuals with experience in mechanics, electronics, and robotics who want to work on physical AI bodies.
  • Platform:Physical hardware, Linux, ROS2 and simulation environment.
  • Usage:Prepare the structure, electronic control and computing platform according to modules, and then gradually complete simulation, calibration and safety testing.
  • License:The aggregation layer is GPL-3.0; modules and third-party materials maintain separate licenses.
