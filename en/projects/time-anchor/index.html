PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Time Anchor is a lightweight tool that adds a "sense of time" to AI companions. After the model call ends, human time continues to flow; this project brings real time back to the next encounter, giving AI the opportunity to distinguish between "still chatting just now" and "coming back after a long time."

Its core is not to allow AI to mechanically tell time every time, but to allow AI to see intervals, confirm time, and bring verified time facts into understanding and response when time may actually change the context.

  • Suitable for:Long-term companionship, diary, return, cross-day, waiting, and any scene that is sensitive to time intervals.
  • Platform:Local Codex / GPT, Claude Code, and Web / official lightweight version.
  • Usage:Select the corresponding version according to the platform you are using. The installation and update instructions are subject to the original project.
  • Boundary:It only provides real time facts and does not pretend that the AI ​​continues to wait when no one calls it, nor does it make up what happened while you were away.
