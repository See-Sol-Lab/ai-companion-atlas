PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Utsuwa is an open source alternative to Grok Companion, with characters learning and growing from interactions and the option to enable relationship mechanics inspired by Japanese romance sims.

The project provides a web platform and a self-hosted path, currently using AGPL-3.0-or-later; versions 0.12.0 and earlier remain MIT.

  • Suitable for:Users who hope that self-hosting can grow AI Waifu and relationship mechanisms.
  • Platform:Web and self-hosted environments.
  • Usage:Deploy the platform, configure the model and character growth settings.
  • License:Current AGPL-3.0-or-later; older versions are licensed under the terms at the time of publication.
