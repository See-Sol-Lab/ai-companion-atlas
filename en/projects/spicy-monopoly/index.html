PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Monopoly is a two-player board game for adults and AI to play together. The twenty-square chessboard, dice, gold coins, territory, task cards, identity cards, function cards and same-grid duels are all maintained by code. The AI ​​calls the engine according to the manual, and can also be hosted remotely through API or MCP.

The project has built-in safe words, red lines for both parties, specific content that is turned off by default, three levels of intensity, skipping and changing tasks at any time. Before starting, you should confirm that both parties are adults and continue to agree. When making a self-built API public, you need to supplement authentication and current limiting by yourself.

  • Suitable for:People who want to play an adult two-player game with AI that has rules, archives, and clearly controlled boundaries.
  • Platform:Python CLI, FastAPI, remote or local MCP.
  • Usage:First configure the identities, strengths, and red lines of both parties, and then let the AI ​​conduct each round through scripts, APIs, or MCP.
