PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

AI Pocket Chat is a set of Android AI companion source code that needs to be compiled by yourself. Chat, character status and relationship data are all saved on your phone, without the need for developers to provide accounts or backend services. It does not come with its own AI model and requires users to fill in their own model interface and key.

Characters have their own schedules, emotions and memories, and will take the initiative to send messages, write diaries, post to Moments, send gifts and make phone calls. The app also includes interactive stories, pets, red envelopes and a small world that can be explored, making getting along with each other more than just chatting.

  • Suitable for:People who want to run their own AI companion on an Android phone and are willing to compile the application themselves.
  • can do:Chat, manage memory, active interaction, friend circle, diary, voice call and interactive story.
  • requires:You need a mobile phone with Android 10 or above, compile the source code yourself, and prepare your own AI model interface.
  • Data boundaries:Chat and relationship data are saved on the phone; when a reply is generated, the necessary content is still sent to the user-configured model service.
