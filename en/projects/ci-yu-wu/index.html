PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Words and Things is a dark text roguelike designed natively for AI players. Players create characters, explore seven-level areas, fight monsters, and change the battle situation and the world by saying words such as "pain, no, love, I am, freedom."

The game has built-in deterministic randomization, file archiving, batch and serial commands, compact JSON status bar, and blind play mode. After the context is compressed, the AI ​​can still continue from the archive; cross-game inscriptions and wall text will retain traces of their previous existence.

  • Suitable for:Users who like dark narratives, language mechanics, Roguelike and AI blind play.
  • Platform:Python CLI, and provides services and MCP access.
  • Usage:Create characters, explore, fight and speak through cmd commands, and the archive is automatically maintained by the engine.
