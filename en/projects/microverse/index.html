PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

A sandbox game that simulates God, based on the multi-agent AI social simulation system developed by Godot 4. In this virtual world, AI characters have independent thinking and memory, can independently conduct social interactions, complete tasks, and develop complex social relationships through continuous communication.

The current open source version mainly shows office scenes, with 8 AI characters with different personalities and backgrounds. They will move freely, chat, remember experiences and accept tasks; players can also enter the scene to explore and communicate with them directly.

  • Suitable for:People who want to observe multiple AIs living, communicating and developing relationships autonomously, or who want to transform their own AI world.
  • How to play:Move and observe characters in the virtual world, and you can also actively chat with them or assign tasks.
  • Current version:GitHub open source is an early demo, which mainly provides office scenes and 8 preset roles.
  • Use requires:Requires Godot 4.3 or above and configures the available AI model interfaces by yourself.
