PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Xin Tong Nian jointly deploys the original heart drive engine and the Ombre Brain memory bank to preserve the continuous mental state and form a closed loop of desires, thoughts, active connections and long-term memory.

The heartbeat directory retains MIT, and the joint distribution root directory is marked AGPL-3.0; Ombre Brain is partially based on the original P0luz project and Yinglianchun fork, and continues to be subject to upstream attribution and non-commercial boundaries.

  • Suitable for:Non-commercial users who want to combine active drive and Ombre Brain memory with one click.
  • Content tips:Including adult-oriented mechanics such as desire drives, Atlas is classified as 18+.
  • Platform:Docker self-hosting.
  • Usage:Recursively clone the submodule and configure two services and Runtime Bridge.
