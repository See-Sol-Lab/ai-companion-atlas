PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

MochiBot is a lightweight self-hosted companion with long-term memory and personality that grows with chats, and proactive connections and expressions of curiosity through Heartbeat.

It also provides habit check-ins, to-dos, precise reminders, diet, weather, Internet search, Telegram emoticons, and WeChat and Telegram channels.

  • Suitable for:Users who want a low-threshold self-hosted long-term companion.
  • Platform:Python, self-hosting, Telegram and WeChat.
  • Usage:Deploy services, configure models, channels, and active frequencies.
  • License:MIT。
