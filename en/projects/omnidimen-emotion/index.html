PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

OmniDimen Emotion is a series of emotion-specific language models based on Qwen2.5/3 fine-tuning, focusing on emotion recognition and emotion-rich text generation. Projects are available in multiple model sizes, as well as GGUF formats such as FP16 and Q4_K_M.

Weights are available through Transformers, SGLang, vLLM, LM Studio, Ollama, or PocketPal. It has a narrower range of capabilities than a universal base, and you should read the license, restrictions, and sensitive content instructions in the corresponding Hugging Face model card before deployment.

  • Suitable for:People who want to run emotion-specific models locally or on edge devices and plug into their own companion systems.
  • Platform:Transformers、SGLang、vLLM、LM Studio、Ollama、PocketPal。
  • Usage:Select the appropriate size and format from Hugging Face, and then deploy it through local inference tools or compatible APIs.
  • License:The model is based on Qwen2.5/3 fine-tuning; the specific weight permission and usage restrictions are subject to each model card.
