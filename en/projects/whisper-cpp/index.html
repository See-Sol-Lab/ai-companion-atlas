Convert what you say into text directly on the device, so that the AI companion can understand the speech without uploading the recording.

After a human speaks or sends a recording, whisper.cpp will run Whisper on the current device to convert the sound into text. The companion front-end or chat model then reads these texts and responds, and the recording does not need to be uploaded to the cloud for recognition first.

PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

whisper.cpp is a native speech-to-text engine that can be embedded in other projects. It receives a recording file or microphone sound, runs Whisper on the current device, and outputs the recognized text.

After giving these texts to your AI companion, he or she can reply based on what you say. Speech recognition can run offline on computers, mobile phones, Raspberry Pis and browsers, making it suitable for people who don't want to hand over recordings to the cloud for processing.

  • Suitable for:People who want to add local voice input to AI companions, or need to organize recorded text offline.
  • Input:Recording file or live microphone sound.
  • Output:The recognized text can be passed on to chat models, subtitle systems or other applications.
  • Usage:Install or compile whisper.cpp, download the compatible Whisper model, and then call the speech recognition capabilities by other applications.
