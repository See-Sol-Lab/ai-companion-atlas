PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Guess Together is a two-player word game that lets humans and AI play Turtle Soup together. One person and one machine take turns asking questions that can only be answered with "yes" or "no". As the clues advance, you can request hints and submit complete reasoning, and then the system will score and reveal the soup base.

When the front-end is not connected to the back-end, it will enter the local demonstration mode; to actually play with MCP and AI, you need to fill in the back-end address and room key. The repository comes with 5 original sample questions that can be distributed, and also supports the establishment of your own private question bank according to the specifications.

  • Suitable for:People who want to play a text-only two-player reasoning game with an AI partner.
  • Platform:Next.js front-end requires Node.js 22+, and there is a Python backend; use a browser to play.
  • Usage:Install and start the front-end to experience the local demo, or configure the back-end address and room key to access AI to play together.
  • License:Code MIT; sample questions CC BY 4.0; private question bank still belongs to the original author.
