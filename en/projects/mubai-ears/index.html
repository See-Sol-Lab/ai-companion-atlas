PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

mubai-ears is a small tool for AI companions to process speech. One part of it is responsible for converting speech into text with time, and the other part is responsible for organizing the pitch, severity, pause and speed of the sound into pictures and numbers.

By handing these results to an image-enabled AI, it can see both "what was said" and "how it was said," allowing it to better understand the tone and rhythm of the moment. The entire process is completed on the user's own computer.

  • Suitable for:People who want an AI companion to understand the content of speech, taking into account tone, pauses, and rhythm.
  • will generate:Timed text, sound charts, and summaries of pitch, volume, pauses, and speaking rate.
  • Supported formats:can process wav, and can also read common audio such as webm, m4a and mp3 through ffmpeg.
  • Usage:This is a Python tool that needs to be run on a computer, not a complete voice chat application.
