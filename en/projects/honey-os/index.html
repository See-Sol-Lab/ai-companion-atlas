PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

HoneyOS is an AI companion that can run on your own computer. It will save your name and personality, your relationship, shared experiences and unfinished things respectively, and you can continue to connect after changing the window, restarting or changing the model.

It can not only chat, but also search for information, write code and use various tools. Local web pages, WeChat and Feishu can connect to the same your AI companion. The data is primarily saved on your computer; when using online models, the necessary dialogue is still sent to the model service you choose.

  • Suitable for:I want a your AI companion who can accompany me for a long time and can actually use tools to complete tasks.
  • Remember:Preserve their identity, your relationship, shared experiences, and unfinished business.
  • can do:Search for information, write code, use Skills and MCP, and create local projects.
  • Project source:Based on the transformation of Hermes Agent, the focus is to enable Agents who can do things to maintain long-term partnerships.
