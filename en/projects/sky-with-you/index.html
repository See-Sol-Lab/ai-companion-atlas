PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

sky-with-you is a PC light-meets-AI play control system. It continuously obtains game screenshots and OCR information, allowing the model to decide to chat, move, act, invite, hold hands, or go home based on the current screen.

The instructions are ultimately executed by Arduino HID to simulate a physical keyboard to avoid pure software injection. The project requires users to prepare hardware, calibrate buttons, and maintain supervision of automatic operations.

  • Suitable for:People who want AI companions to actually enter Light Encounter and conduct joint play experiments.
  • Platform:Windows PC Light Encounter + Arduino HID Device.
  • Usage:configured the vision,model and Arduino execution chain, calibrated the keys,and ran them under supervision.
  • License:The source code of the repository is open to the public, and the specific reuse and distribution are subject to the project statement.
