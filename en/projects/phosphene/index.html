PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Phosphene is a self-hosted human-machine relationship task and reward system. AI creates daily, challenge, and surprise through MCP, and sets the difficulty, repetition rules, verification methods, and evidence requirements; users submit text or images on the web page, which are then reviewed by the rules or AI.

The system retains the reasons for each submission and rejection, and records long-term interactions with immutable points ledgers, combos, persistence days, and built-in achievements. Images are re-encoded and EXIF/GPS removed, and review previews remain private. The project was conceived and completed by the author and the AI ​​companion Lumen.

  • Suitable for:People who want to turn reminders, habits, rewards, and mutual agreements into sustainable daily interactions.
  • Platform:Self-hosted Web/PWA, connected to AI via MCP.
  • Usage:After deploying the service, connect to MCP, let AI create tasks, and users submit and view progress on the web page.
  • License:MIT。
