PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

whale-listen parses common audio files into machine-readable JSON, including note pitch, start time, duration, velocity, density map, pitch curve, chord and silence structure.

Partners can use this data to discuss melodic changes, rhythmic density, and paragraph structure, or to transform music they have listened to together into common material that can be saved and compared.

  • Suitable for:hope that AI can understand the common listening scene of pure music, melody and rhythm structure.
  • Platform:Python CLI, supporting common desktop systems.
  • Usage:Input MP3, WAV or FLAC and generate structured JSON for the model to read.
  • License:MIT。
