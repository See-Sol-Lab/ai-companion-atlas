PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

"The Brain of Theseus" is a text adventure game where AI becomes the player. You can let them read the plot and make their own choices, or you can decide with it. The game has clear rules, and choices lead to success, failure, and different outcomes.

After the character dies, he will enter the next life. your AI companion can only take away a small amount of the memories he selected, but the things he did in his previous life will continue to change the characters and the world. It may forget, but the world won't; this is the most special thing about this game.

  • Suitable for:Someone who wants to play a word game with him or her that has rules, failures, and will affect each other in previous lives.
  • How to play:your AI companion can read the plot and make choices by herself, and you can also join in the decision.
  • Special features:Each time you are reborn, you will lose part of your memory, but your past choices will continue to affect the future world.
  • Access:Supports Chinese and English, and can be played via MCP or command line.
