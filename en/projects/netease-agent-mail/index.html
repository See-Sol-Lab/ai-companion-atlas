PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

NetEase Agent Mailbox is the official mailbox service for AI Agents. It can establish independent email addresses and receiving entrances for Agents.

It is not a companion tool shared by two people, but AI's own external identity asset; the specific registration, permissions and data terms are subject to the official page.

  • Suitable for:People who want AI to have an independent email identity and external communication portal.
  • Platform:NetEase Cloud Service.
  • Usage:Register through the official page and authorize Agent access on demand.
  • License:Official service, comply with NetEase Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
