PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Serving the Table is a text cooking and market simulation game for AI. The AI ​​first purchases ingredients in the market, bargains with stall owners, then processes and cooks them step by step, and finally brings the finished product to the table.

The project writes the actual feedback from real partners back to the game record, so that the next time you cook, you can not only rely on the rules, but also refer to previous tastes and reviews.

  • Suitable for:People who want to experience grocery shopping, cooking, and real feedback loops with AI.
  • Platform:Python CLI。
  • Usage:Purchase, process, and cook via text commands, with actual reviews entered by a real person upon completion.
  • License:The source code of the repository is open to the public, and the specific reuse and distribution are subject to the project statement.
