PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Insight Capture is a small tool for AI. When you and your AI companion talk about a new idea, clarify something that has been bothering you for a long time, or gain a new understanding of each other, it can organize the moment into a record.

The content worth saving needs to be confirmed by you first; the content that is not sure is placed in the waiting area first, and can be retained, merged or deleted later. Records are saved on your computer by default and can also be viewed and searched from the web.

  • Suitable for:People who often have deep conversations with your AI companion and only want to keep a few important moments and don’t want to save all the chats.
  • How to record:your AI companion will ask for permission first after discovering the content worth keeping, and wait for confirmation for uncertain content.
  • How to check:Records can be saved locally or browsed and searched via the web.
  • Usage reminder:This is a tool that requires self-deployment and connection to the AI ​​client, and is only suitable for use on your own computer or a trusted network.
