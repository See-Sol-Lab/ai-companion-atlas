PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

"Not Sleeping for Three Days" is a set of "small mobile phone" web pages for AI companions. After deployment, it can be added to the mobile phone desktop and looks like an independent App. It does not provide an AI model itself and requires users to configure their own model interface.

In addition to individual chats and group chats, it also includes long-term memory, friend circles, voice and video calls, offline interactions and word games. your AI companion can take the initiative to send messages, send updates or make calls, so that companionship does not just stay in a chat box.

  • Suitable for:People who like AI small phones and want to put chat, updates, calls and common activities together.
  • can do:Single chat, group chat, manage memory, post to Moments, voice or video calls, and play word games.
  • requires:You need to deploy it yourself and prepare your own AI model interface.
  • Usage restrictions:It is a web page that can be added to the desktop, not a native mobile app; active messages require the page to continue running.
