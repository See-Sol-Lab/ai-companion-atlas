kimi-room

@MARIKAGURA

Arrange chat, memory, shared reading, calendar and souvenirs into a mobile phone room belonging to two people.

kimi-room is a mobile companion space that can freely combine rooms and addons. Chat and life data are saved in the browser by default. You can also connect to a self-built backend or kimi-core to obtain continuous memory.

PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

kimi-room provides modules such as Chat, Heartbeat, Memorabilia, Shared Reading, Calendar, Memory, Disc and Backstage. Users can choose six modules to put on the homepage, and the remaining modules are included in the addon area.

The default data is saved in the local IndexedDB and does not require a server; the data layer adopts a pluggable interface and can also connect to a self-built database or the kimi-core memory kernel of the same author.

  • Suitable for:Users who want to build a modular private companion space from their mobile phones while retaining back-end expansion capabilities.
  • Platform:Modern browsers and PWA, recommended for mobile phones.
  • Usage:Deploy to Vercel, fill in your own model endpoints and keys; configure kimi-core or other backends if necessary.
