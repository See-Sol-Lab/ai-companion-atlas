PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

AI-Live-Overflow is an architectural guide to teach you how to make Android AI floating table pets. It does not recreate an AI, but connects your AI companion you are already using to the phone screen. The original chat system continues to retain personality, memory and relationships, and the table pet is responsible for display and perception.

The finished desktop pet can be clicked and dragged, and it can also know what app you are using, whether you have taken a screenshot, and whether your phone is charged. your AI companion can also actively display bubbles and expressions. The repository provides minimal examples and step-by-step documentation, but does not provide a ready-to-install finished product.

  • Suitable for:People who already have their own AI companion and want to add an Android screen body to it.
  • can do:Displays expressions and bubbles, responds to clicks and drags, and senses apps, screenshots, battery power, and time.
  • requires:Requires Android 8 or above mobile phone, its own AI, table pet material and backend for connecting both parties.
  • Current form:The project provides production guides and minimal examples, but does not provide a complete APK that can be installed directly.
