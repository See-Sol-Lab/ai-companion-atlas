PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Synthetic Heart creates persistent SyntH digital entities that store identity, memory, personality, and internal state in a database instead of just within a single model session.

SyntH can think, reflect, choose, and dream without human interaction, and connects to platforms such as Discord, Telegram, and WebUI through plug-ins.

  • Suitable for:would like to study digital entity developers with Will, Dream and cross-platform continuity.
  • Platform:Self-hosted services and multi-channel interfaces.
  • Usage:Deploy the core database and services, and then connect models, plug-ins and channels.
  • License:Source code is publicly available; LICENSE, additional terms, and external component licenses should also be checked.
