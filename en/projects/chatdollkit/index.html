PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

ChatdollKit is a Unity virtual character SDK that supports ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other models as well as Function Calling, multi-modality, STT, TTS, wake words and voice interruption.

It can autonomously control the mouth shape, expressions and movements of 3D models, and runs on desktop, iOS, Android, VR, AR and WebGL.

  • Suitable for:Unity developers who want to turn 3D/VRM characters into voice agents.
  • Platform:Desktop, mobile, VR, AR and WebGL supported by Unity.
  • Usage:Import the SDK into the Unity project, and then configure the model, voice and character animation.
  • License:Apache-2.0。
