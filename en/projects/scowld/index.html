PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Scowld is a native iOS AI voice companion. It supports text and voice chat, front-facing camera visual context, hands-free wake-up, locally saved historical conversations, and VRM characters with lip-syncs, standby actions, and expression changes.

Users can connect to Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Ollama, Groq, OpenRouter and other models, and configure speech recognition and speech synthesis services respectively. The API Key is saved in the iOS Keychain, and the conversation record and selected context are saved locally on the device. The project's character rendering frontend uses and is credited to the MIT-licensed Amica/Arbius AI.

  • Suitable for:People who want integrated voice, visual, and animated character companionship on their iPhone.
  • Platform:iOS 17.0+; building your own requires Xcode 16+.
  • Usage:Install from the App Store or source code, fill in your own model, STT and TTS keys in the settings.
  • License:MIT; Third-party models and voice services are still used according to their respective terms.
