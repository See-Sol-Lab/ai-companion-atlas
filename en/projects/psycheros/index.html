PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Psycheros saves first-person identity files such as self, user, and relationship through the entity-core MCP service, and summarizes daily memories into weekly, monthly, and year levels.

The same entity can be called by multiple MCP front-ends. Chat history such as ChatGPT, Claude, SillyTavern, Letta, etc. can also be converted into import packages through entity-loom, and Pulse can be used to trigger actions independently.

  • Suitable for:People who want the same AI entity to maintain identity continuity across multiple frontends and bodies.
  • Platform:Deno, MCP and multiple compatible clients.
  • Usage:Deploy entity-core, initialize identity files and connect required Embodiment.
