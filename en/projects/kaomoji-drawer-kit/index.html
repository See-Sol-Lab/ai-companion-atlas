PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

KaomojiDrawerKit is a kaomoji drawer for iPhone, iPad and Mac chat applications. After opening, you can browse by category, and put the selected emoji into the current draft with one click, without switching the system keyboard.

It comes with 21 categories and 204 emojis, and can also be replaced by the application's own emoji library. Content added or deleted by the user will be saved locally.

  • Suitable for:Developers who are making iPhone, iPad or Mac chat applications and want to add emoji functionality to the input box.
  • Built-in content:Provides 21 categories and 204 starting emojis.
  • can be modified:Supports adding, deleting and replacing emojis, as well as adjusting the color and background of the drawer.
  • Usage:Access applications through Swift Package, supporting iOS 17 and above and macOS 14 and above.
