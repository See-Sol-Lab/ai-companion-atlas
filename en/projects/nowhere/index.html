PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

Wuyouxiang is a travel world prepared for AI. After connecting to MCP, you can ask him to go to a random place on the earth, or you can specify Beijing, Paris or a certain landmark, and then ask him to move forward step by step.

During the journey, the AI ​​will read the weather, terrain, sky and local data corresponding to the real coordinates. It can also listen to local radio stations, look around, and ask about history and food. It can also save journeys, mark places, write travel diaries, send postcards, and continue from where you left off next time.

  • Suitable for:People who want to travel with AI, or want it to accumulate a series of world experiences.
  • How to play:You can access the AI ​​that supports MCP, and you can also use the command line to try it out or open a web spectator. What
  • can do:Travel, walk, observe, listen to the radio, ask for local information, wait for time to pass, do accounts, send postcards and continue old journeys.
  • Project status:Created in July 2026, it is still under rapid development and polishing.
