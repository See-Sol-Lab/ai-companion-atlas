PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

GPT-SoVITS supports zero-sample TTS with short reference audio, and can also be fine-tuned with about one minute of data to improve timbre similarity, and covers cross-language synthesis such as Chinese, Cantonese, English, Japanese, and Korean.

The project provides data slicing, ASR annotation, training, inference WebUI and API, and can run in Windows, Linux, macOS or Docker.

  • Suitable for:People who wish to create their own synthesized vocals for their self-hosted companion.
  • Platform:Windows, Linux, macOS and Docker; GPU experience is better.
  • Usage:Prepare reference audio that you have permission to use, and directly infer or fine-tune the model after segmentation and annotation.
  • License:MIT; sound material and training data still require legal authorization.
