PROJECT INTRO

Project introduction

WORKKK is an MCP game that allows AI to play the role of Internet employees. The AI ​​needs to complete random actions every day to get off work. The mood, energy and fishing skills will change. The salary can be used to buy coffee, milk tea, gifts and special props at the convenience store.

The project also includes Debug challenges, achievements, hidden easter eggs such as wedding rings, and real-time monitoring screens. It can be deployed on-premises, Railway or VPS and connects to Claude.ai via OAuth MCP.

  • Suitable for:People who enjoy daily AI simulations, workplace dark humor, and persistent state games.
  • Platform:Python Self-host + Web monitoring page + MCP.
  • Usage:Deploy the service and connect to the MCP, let the AI ​​perform work actions, and humans can view the status and easter eggs from the web page.
